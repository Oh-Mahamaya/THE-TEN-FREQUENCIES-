What is the Das Mahavidya?

The Das Mahavidya — the Ten Mahavidyas — are ten forms of the Goddess in Tantra, each a different face of Shakti. Maa Kali is the first. This book, and the Ten Frequencies series, moves through all ten — one book per Mahavidya.
